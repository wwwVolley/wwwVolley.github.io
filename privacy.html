LIMECODE PRIVACY

Your files
stay yours.

Last updated: October 2026

LimeCode is designed as a local macOS editor. The app does not require an account and does not upload the contents of documents to a LimeCode server.

Files are accessed when you select them through macOS Open or Save panels. Unsaved recovery drafts and language preferences are stored locally on your Mac. Optional donations are processed by Apple through StoreKit; LimeCode does not receive your payment details.

The website edition's CLI and MCP tools communicate with a locally running LimeCode instance. They are distributed separately and are not included in the App Store edition.

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the public contact details listed on the wwwVolley website.
